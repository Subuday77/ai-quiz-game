About AI Quiz Game

A little curiosity can take you a long way.

A game for curious minds

AI Quiz Game is an independent trivia project for people who enjoy discovering new things, testing their knowledge, and sharing the excitement of a good question.

How it works

Explore questions from a wide range of subjects, choose from four answers, and progress through a round at your own pace. Different game modes, earned stars, and an optional bonus challenge keep each round interesting.

Learning, play, and improvement

Artificial intelligence helps create the questions, while quality checks and player feedback help us improve the experience. We aim to make learning feel rewarding, approachable, and fun.

The project continues to grow, one question at a time. Thanks for being part of it.
